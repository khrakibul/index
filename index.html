About Me

A brief introduction to my academic background and research interests.

I am currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Dhaka University of Engineering & Technology (DUET), Gazipur, with a research focus on Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, and Medical Image Processing.

I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from DUET with a CGPA of 3.85/4.00, securing First Class First position in my batch. My undergraduate thesis focused on improving polyp segmentation in endoscopic images using attention-enhanced deep learning models.

My current research interests include medical image segmentation, multimodal learning, vision-language models, and efficient deep learning architectures for medical applications.
