About

I am a Computer Science researcher currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Dhaka University of Engineering & Technology (DUET), Gazipur.

My research interests lie primarily in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and Medical Image Processing, with a particular focus on medical image segmentation and multimodal AI.

I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from DUET with a CGPA of 3.85/4.00, securing First Class First position in my batch and receiving the Gold Medal. I was also awarded the Dean's Award for Academic Excellence three times.

My undergraduate thesis focused on polyp segmentation in endoscopic images using attention-enhanced U-Net architectures.
