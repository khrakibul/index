About Me

Who I Am

I am a Computer Science researcher currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Dhaka University of Engineering & Technology (DUET), Gazipur, Bangladesh.

My academic and research interests focus on Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and Medical Image Processing, with a particular interest in medical image segmentation and multimodal AI.

I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from DUET with a CGPA of 3.85/4.00, securing First Class First position in my batch and receiving the Gold Medal.

During my undergraduate study, I worked on polyp segmentation in endoscopic images using attention-enhanced U-Net architectures. This work led to a peer-reviewed conference publication in IEEE Xplore.

I am currently interested in developing efficient and trustworthy AI systems for medical image understanding, segmentation, and multimodal image-text learning.

Current Academic Focus

Developing AI-based solutions for medical image segmentation and multimodal understanding, including vision-language models and efficient deep learning architectures.
